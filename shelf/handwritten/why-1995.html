Why a 1995-like website in 2026

Performance

Loading a 60KB JavaScript library and using fancy CSS frameworks is too slow even for "modern" browsers. Plain HTML renders instantaneously on anything.

Aesthetics

If aesthetics were more important than knowledge, then the Internet Archive or Wikipedia would have failed at launch.

They didn't, because "Knowledge is better than looks."

No templates?

Be original, don't copy the same layout as 1,000 other Fortune 500 companies. When everyone uses the exact same template, every site blends together and loses its identity.
