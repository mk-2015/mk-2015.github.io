NAME
       operator - C operator precedence and order of evaluation

DESCRIPTION
       This manual page lists C operators and their precedence in evaluation.

       lb  lb  lb  l  l  l.   Operator  Associativity  Notes  []  ()  .  -> ++
       --    left to right   [1] ++ -- & * + - ~ ! sizeof right  to  left  [2]
       (type)    right  to  left * / %     left to right + -  left to right <<
       >>     left to right < > <= >= left to right ==  !=     left  to  right
       &    left  to  right ^    left to right |    left to right &&   left to
       right ||   left to right ?:   right to left = *= /= %= += -= <<= >>= &=
       ^= |=   right to left ,    left to right

       The following notes provide further information to the above table:

       [1]  The ++ and -- operators at this precedence level are  the  postfix
            flavors of the operators.
       [2]  The  ++  and  -- operators at this precedence level are the prefix
            flavors of the operators.
