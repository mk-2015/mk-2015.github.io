NAME
       pnmcat - replaced by pamcat


DESCRIPTION
       This program is part of Netpbm(1).

       pnmcat  was  obsoleted by pamcat(1), introduced with Netpbm 11.00 (Sep‐
       tember 2022).  pamcat is backward compatible  with  pnmcat,  plus  adds
       many additional functions, including the ability to process PAM images.

       In  Netpbm  before  11.00, use the manual for pamcat with pnmcat.  Fea‐
       tures that are in pamcat but not pnmcat  are  indicated  by  statements
       that they didn't exist before 11.00.
