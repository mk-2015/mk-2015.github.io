NAME
       ts_print, ts_print_raw - A very basic test routine for tslib.


SYNOPSIS
       ts_print

       ts_print_raw


DESCRIPTION
       ts_print  simply  reads tslib input samples and prints them to the con‐
       sole. With ts_print , the configured filters are applied,  see  ts.conf
       (5). With ts_print_raw , no filters are applied.

       It supports single touch only.

SEE ALSO
       ts.conf (5), ts_test (1), ts_test_mt (1), ts_calibrate (1)
