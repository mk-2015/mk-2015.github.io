NAME
       ts_harvest - Harvest hundreds of raw touch screen coordinates.

SYNOPSIS
       ts_harvest

DESCRIPTION
       ts_harvest  can  be used to linearize non linear touch screens as found
       in the h2200 device. Touch screen  coordinates  are  saved  to  ts_har‐
       vest.out

SEE ALSO
       ts.conf (5), ts_test (1), ts_test_mt (1), ts_calibrate (1)
