NAME
       jpeg_extract - jpeg extractor

SYNOPSIS
       jpeg_extract <filename>

DESCRIPTION
       jpeg_extractor  is  used by fiwalk, provided by TSK (The Sleuth Kit) to
       manipulate JPG files.
