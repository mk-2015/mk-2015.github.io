NAME
       pnmfile - replaced by pamfile


DESCRIPTION
       This program is part of Netpbm(1).

       pnmfile was replaced in Netpbm 10.9 (September 2002) by pamfile(1).

       pamfile  is  backward  compatible with pnmfile, but works on PAM images
       too.
