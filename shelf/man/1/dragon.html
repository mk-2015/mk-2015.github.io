NAME
       dragon - a video player that has a usability focus

SYNOPSIS


       dragon [URL]

DESCRIPTION
       dragon player plays audio and video in different formats.

OPTIONS
       --play-dvd
           Play DVD Video.

           help:/dragonplayer (either enter this URL into Konqueror, or run
           khelpcenter help:/dragonplayer).
